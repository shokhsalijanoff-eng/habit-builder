# Habit Builder — User Personas

## Persona 1: University Student

**Name:** Min-Jun  
**Age:** 21  
**Occupation:** University Student  
**Technical Level:** Basic

### Background

Min-Jun is a university student who wants to build and maintain consistent daily habits such as studying, exercising, reading, and other personal routines.

### Goals

- Build consistent daily habits.
- Keep track of completed and incomplete habits.
- Quickly see overall progress.
- Use a simple productivity tool without unnecessary complexity.

### Problems

- It can be difficult to remember which habits have been completed.
- Managing several habits at the same time can become confusing.
- Min-Jun needs a simple way to see overall progress.

### Needs from Habit Builder

- Add new habits easily.
- View all current habits.
- Mark habits as completed.
- Delete unnecessary habits.
- See progress statistics.

### User Story

> As a university student, I want to add and complete my daily habits so that I can build a consistent routine.

## Persona 2: Busy Young Professional

**Name:** Daniel  
**Age:** 27  
**Occupation:** Office Worker  
**Technical Level:** Intermediate

### Background

Daniel has a busy work schedule and wants to maintain personal habits such as exercising, reading, and other activities outside of work.

### Goals

- Maintain a consistent personal routine.
- Quickly check habit progress.
- Keep track of completed and remaining habits.
- Manage habits from a simple dashboard.

### Problems

- A busy schedule can make it difficult to maintain personal habits.
- Daniel needs a quick way to check his progress.
- Complex productivity applications can contain unnecessary features.

### Needs from Habit Builder

- See all habits in one place.
- Mark completed habits.
- Delete habits that are no longer needed.
- See total, completed, and remaining habits.
- View overall completion percentage.

### User Story

> As a busy professional, I want to see my overall habit progress so that I can quickly understand how well I am following my routine.
